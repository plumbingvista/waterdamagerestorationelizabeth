Wet Basement in Elizabeth? The First 60 Minutes

What you do in the first hour decides how much of your home can be saved. Follow these steps:

1. Stop the water — shut the main valve if a pipe burst, and switch off power to wet rooms.
2. Protect valuables — move rugs, electronics, furniture and documents off wet floors first.
3. Take photos — document everything before you touch it; your insurance claim will need them.
4. Start extraction — mop, towels, a wet/dry vac. Standing water grows mold within 24–48 hours.
5. Get air moving — fans and open windows on the dry side of the home.
6. Call professionals the same day — hidden moisture inside walls causes mold and rot weeks later. Call +1 (908) 509-3796.
